Initial public release of `@arki/expr` (safe formula parsing, printing and evaluation in float, decimal, rational and complex domains, with LaTeX output) and `@arki/cas` (bounded computer algebra on expr: exact polynomials and rational functions with π and e as symbols, `simplify`, `derivative` with a rule-by-rule `derivation`, `factor` over ℚ and closed-form roots).
