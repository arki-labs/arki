'@arki/math': minor
---

Valid input that does not converge now returns `NotConverged` (`math.not_converged`) instead of throwing: `integrate`, `limit`, `derivative` (the numeric one), `findRoot`, `bisect`, `newton`, `findRoots`, `polynomialRoots`, `rank`, `symmetricEigenvalues`, `symmetricEigenDecompose`, `eigenvalues`, `eigenDecompose`, `singularValues`, `singularValueDecompose`, `conditionNumber`, `pseudoInverse` and `leastSquares`. The failure's `cause` is the `ConvergenceError`, which now carries `iterations` (its constructor takes it as a second argument); the root, integral and limit errors still carry `.best`, the last estimate. New `orNotConverged` and the `NotConvergedFailure` type. `significantDigits(text)` returns `InvalidNumber` or `NumberOutOfRange` instead of throwing `NumberFormatError`, and refuses text past 10 000 digits. `parseNumber`, `parseDecimal` and `significantDigits` scan in linear time: a run of 200 000 spaces or zeros no longer takes seconds. `polynomialRoots` takes a degree of at most 500 and `Complex.nthRoots` an `n` of at most 1 000 000, or throw `ResourceLimitError`. Results that overflowed to `Infinity` or `NaN` now throw `NumericRangeError`: `round`, `roundTo`, `convert`, the geometry shapes, `distance`, `polygonArea`, `signedPolygonArea`, the solved triangle, and the incomplete gamma and beta functions (so `chiSquare`, `fisherF` and `poisson` cdfs) at huge parameters. `cubicRoots`, `quadraticRoots`, `polynomialRoots` and `betaLn` throw `NumericRangeError`, not `InvalidArgumentError`, when a root or sum overflows. A malformed locale tag in `format*`, `parseNumber`, `parseDecimal`, `localeNumberSymbols`, `formatUnit` and the number words falls back to `en-US` and is logged at DEBUG. `@arki/log` is a new runtime dependency.
