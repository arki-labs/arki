Welcome to [Ekuation](/) ("Ekuation", "we", "us", "our"). We build free online
calculators for finance, health, math, and everyday life. This Privacy Policy
explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

**The short version:** we collect as little data as possible, we never sell your
data, and we use privacy-friendly analytics that don't track you across the web.

## What We Collect

### Information You Give Us

If you create an account, we store:

- **Account details** — your name, email address, and profile picture (if you
  sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook, we receive these from your provider).
- **Calculator profiles** — personal details you choose to save (such as age,
  height, weight, activity level, or currency preference) so your calculators
  can auto-fill. You control what you save, and you can delete your profiles at
  any time.
- **Field preferences** — your per-calculator display settings (e.g. which unit
  system to default to).

You do not need an account to use our calculators. If you use Ekuation without
signing in, your recently used calculators and any profile data are stored
locally in your browser (localStorage) and never sent to our servers.

### Information We Collect Automatically

- **Privacy-friendly analytics** — we use [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io),
  a lightweight, cookie-free analytics tool. Plausible does not use cookies, does
  not track you across sites, and does not collect personal information. It tells
  us aggregate page view counts — nothing more.
- **Error reports** — we may use [Sentry](https://sentry.io) to capture
  JavaScript errors so we can fix bugs. Error reports may include your browser
  type, operating system, and the page URL where the error occurred. They do not
  include personal information.
- **Session recordings** — when enabled, we may use
  [Microsoft Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com) to understand how people
  interact with our pages (clicks, scrolls, navigation). Clarity anonymizes data
  and does not identify individual users.

### Cookies

We do not set any first-party tracking cookies. If you sign in, we use a
session cookie strictly to keep you logged in. Third-party tools we integrate
(such as Clarity or Google AdSense, when enabled) may set their own cookies —
see their respective privacy policies for details.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide the service** — run calculators, save your profiles, and keep you
  signed in.
- **Improve the product** — understand which calculators are popular and fix
  errors.
- **Communicate with you** — send transactional emails (e.g. magic link sign-in)
  via [Resend](https://resend.com). We do not send marketing emails.

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data
brokers.

## AI Features

Some calculators offer an optional AI-powered chat that explains your results.
When you use this feature, your calculation inputs and results are sent to
[OpenAI](https://openai.com) to generate a response. We do not send your
account details or profile data to OpenAI.

## Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of third-party services to run Ekuation:

| Service | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Plausible | Privacy-friendly analytics (no cookies, no personal data) |
| Sentry | Error tracking |
| Microsoft Clarity | Session recordings (when enabled) |
| Google AdSense | Advertising (when enabled) |
| OpenAI | AI chat feature |
| Resend | Transactional email |
| Google / Apple / Facebook | Social sign-in (only if you choose to use it) |

Each service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them if you
have questions about how they handle data.

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers hosted by [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com) in
the European Union. We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and follow standard
security practices to protect your information.

## Your Rights

You can:

- **Access** your data by viewing your account settings and calculator profiles.
- **Delete** your account and all associated data at any time from your account
  settings.
- **Use Ekuation without an account** — no personal data is collected on our
  servers in that case.

If you are in the EU, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the
right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory
authority.

## Children's Privacy

Ekuation is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect
personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with
personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update
the date at the top. Your continued use of Ekuation after changes are posted
means you accept the updated policy.
