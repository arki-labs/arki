# [**arki**](https://www.notion.so/arki-b8e5ad1859f64a79a0c637ebd31b43e3?pvs=21)

**Tagline**: _"Your AI cofounder for the first mile."_

---

**Identity & Role**

- **AI-powered no-code builder** with a **startup-first DNA.**
- **Concept**: Helps founders move from **idea → MVP → pitch-ready product** with built-in tech + AI guidance.
- **Positioning**: Not just a builder, but a **trusted cofounder** who helps you **design, refine, and launch.**
- **Platform Alignment**: Links with BrandOps (Domodoro/Radarama/DancingMail) for naming, monitoring, and launch campaigns; can surface Kapodopera/Tolstoy cultural assets when founders need storytelling fuel.

---

**Core Features (MVP – Founder Partner)**

1. **Natural Language Builder ("Vibe Coding")**
   - Founders describe idea → ARKI generates app structure (UI, DB, flows).
2. **Built-in Infrastructure**
   - Auth, DB, storage, analytics, email → ready from day one.
3. **Refinement Loop (Founder Conversations)**
   - AI acts as a partner: _"What if we pivot to subscriptions?"_
   - Suggests app modifications aligned with startup thinking.
4. **Founder Templates**
   - Prebuilt SaaS, marketplace, booking, and CRM starter kits.
   - Not "generic apps" → but **startup models.**
5. **One-Click Deploy**
   - Web deployment (PWA).
   - Ready to demo to investors or early customers.

  - AI reviews your app → highlights weak spots (scalability, monetization gaps).

  - Every app supports global scaling (auto i18n).
- **Code Export**
  - For technical teams → export to React + Node when scaling.
- **Founder Marketplace**
  - Share/sell templates or even MVPs.

  - Cohort-based sprints pairing founders with mentors, investor office hours, and shared analytics dashboards that track MVP traction.

  - Opt-in directory of vetted builders/designers ready to engage once MVP metrics hit thresholds, enabling smooth transition from no-code to hybrid teams.

  - Generates data-rich update briefs tied to product usage, ready to send to angels/VCs and integrated with BrandOps Pulse.
